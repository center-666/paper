## P2: fixed-amplitude outer-set closure

Close the route from trusted records to onset-indexed continuous amplitude intervals, pollution-set outer approximations, and output action. Keep continuous interval verification, source binding, and unresolved fallback. A finite amplitude grid cannot be used as formal coverage.

## P3: compare geometric projection and directional certificates

On the same certified outer set, compare the exact two-dimensional convex-hull projection with the current direction-supported action. Select the reported algorithm based on its certified guarantee, cost, and reproducibility. The projection theorem remains the minimax benchmark.

## P4: same-information comparisons

Compare Monitor, rejection baselines, a point-estimate compensator, TRSR/replay, and the selected certified recovery method on the same trusted log. Oracle remains a separate upper-bound diagnostic.

## P5: independent experiments

Freeze seeds and protocol before confirmation runs. Report all trajectories, failures, no-action cases, false actions on healthy records, recovery delay, risk-bound slack, query-cost quantiles, and memory.

## P6: manuscript assembly

Write around trusted-history-constrained vector pollution and conditional-risk-safe recovery. The historical results support the theorem foundation and a directional-certificate prototype. They do not support significant overall MSE improvement, full per-step convex-hull projection, finite-bit communication certification, or real-time performance.

## Stop conditions

- If the persistent fixed-amplitude response does not match direct candidate filtering, stop before expanding the audit.
- If only a finite amplitude grid can be implemented, restrict claims to exploratory evidence.
- If the outer pollution set commonly contains the origin or the online certificate arrives after the recovery window, reassess algorithm investment before increasing experiment scale.
